AN-NUR — DO'KON KASSASI
========================

O'RNATISH
---------
1. Node.js o'rnating (agar yo'q bo'lsa): https://nodejs.org/
   — LTS versiyani tanlang (20 yoki 22)

2. An-Nur-0.1.2-Setup.exe ni ishga tushiring
   — Odatda shu papkaga o'rnatiladi:
     C:\Users\SIZNING_ISMINGIZ\AppData\Local\An-Nur

3. O'rnatish tugagach An-Nur-Ornatish.cmd avtomatik ishga tushadi
   — Internet shart emas, hamma narsa paketda bor

4. Bosh ekranda "An-Nur" yorlig'i paydo bo'ladi


ISHLATISH
---------
• Bosh ekran → An-Nur
• Yoki: An-Nur-Ishga-Tushirish.cmd

Brauzerda ochiladi: http://localhost:3000

Kirish:
  Egasi:  admin / admin123
  Kassir: kassir / kassir123

Parolni Sozlamalar → Xodimlar dan o'zgartiring.


MUHIM
-----
• Internet shart emas (offline ishlaydi)
• Ma'lumotlar: data\hisob.db faylida
• Har kuni zaxira oling (Sozlamalar → Zaxira)
